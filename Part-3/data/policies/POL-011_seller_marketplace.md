# Document ID: POL-011
# Title: Seller and Marketplace Policy

Products on the marketplace may be fulfilled or supplied by different sellers.

Seller-specific conditions may affect return, exchange, warranty, delivery, and other order processes.

The seller associated with an order can be identified from the applicable order or product information.

Customers should follow the support process associated with the specific order when a seller-related issue occurs.

Seller policies cannot override mandatory protections or applicable platform processes.

A seller may require product-specific information or evidence when evaluating a support request.

If a seller-related issue cannot be resolved through the normal support flow, the customer may escalate the issue through customer support.
