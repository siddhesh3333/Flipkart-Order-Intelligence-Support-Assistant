# Document ID: POL-008
# Title: Payment Policy

Customers may pay for eligible orders using the payment methods presented during checkout.

Payment availability can depend on the product, seller, customer location, order value, and other transaction conditions.

A payment is considered successful only after the payment status is confirmed by the order system.

Customers should not place a duplicate order solely because a payment confirmation is delayed.

If money is debited but the order is not confirmed, the customer should retain the transaction reference and contact support when required.

A payment issue may require verification with the relevant payment provider.

Refunds for eligible cancelled or returned orders follow the applicable refund process.
