# Document ID: POL-007
# Title: Defective Product Policy

A defective product is a product that does not function or perform as expected under its applicable product conditions.

Customers should report a suspected defect through the order support process.

The support team may request information about the defect, usage conditions, photographs, videos, or other evidence.

The available resolution may depend on the product category, seller conditions, warranty coverage, and applicable return or exchange eligibility.

A defective product may be eligible for replacement, exchange, repair, refund, or another resolution depending on the applicable conditions.

Customers should follow troubleshooting or inspection instructions provided by support before a final resolution is determined.

If the product is covered by a warranty, the warranty process may apply instead of or in addition to the standard return process.
