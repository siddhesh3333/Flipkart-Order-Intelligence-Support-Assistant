# Document ID: POL-006
# Title: Damaged or Incorrect Product Policy

A customer who receives a visibly damaged product should report the issue through the applicable order support flow as soon as possible.

A customer who receives a product that is different from the product ordered should also report the issue through the applicable order support flow as soon as possible.

The customer should preserve the product, packaging, accessories, and other relevant materials until the support process provides further instructions.

Photos or other evidence of the damage or incorrect product may be requested during the support process.

The resolution may involve return, replacement, exchange, refund, or another applicable remedy depending on the order and product conditions.

A damaged-product or incorrect-product request may require verification before a final resolution is approved.

Customers should not discard the damaged or incorrectly delivered product or packaging before receiving instructions when a verification process may be required.

The applicable resolution depends on product eligibility and the circumstances of the reported issue.
