# Document ID: POL-006
# Title: Damaged Product Policy

A customer who receives a visibly damaged product should report the issue through the applicable order support flow as soon as possible.

The customer should preserve the product, packaging, accessories, and other relevant materials until the support process provides further instructions.

Photos or other evidence of the damage may be requested during the support process.

The resolution may involve return, replacement, exchange, refund, or another applicable remedy depending on the order and product conditions.

A damaged-product request may require verification before a final resolution is approved.

Customers should not discard the damaged product or packaging before receiving instructions when a verification process may be required.

The applicable resolution depends on product eligibility and the circumstances of the reported damage.
