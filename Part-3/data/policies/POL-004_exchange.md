# Document ID: POL-004
# Title: Exchange Policy

An exchange is available only when the product and order are eligible for exchange.

Exchange eligibility can depend on product category, seller conditions, inventory availability, and the reason for the exchange.

A customer should submit an exchange request through the applicable order support flow within the permitted period.

The replacement product may be subject to availability at the time the exchange is processed.

If the requested replacement is unavailable, the customer may be offered another eligible resolution according to the order conditions.

The original product may need to be collected before or during fulfillment of the replacement.

An exchange request can be rejected when the order or product does not satisfy the applicable exchange conditions.
