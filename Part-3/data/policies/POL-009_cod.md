# Document ID: POL-009
# Title: Cash on Delivery Policy

Cash on Delivery, or COD, is available only for orders and locations where the payment method is offered.

COD availability may depend on product, seller, delivery location, order value, and other eligibility conditions.

The customer is expected to pay the applicable amount when the order is delivered.

The customer should keep the required payment amount ready for a COD delivery.

A COD order may be subject to additional order or verification conditions.

If COD is not displayed during checkout, the order is not currently eligible for COD under the available payment options.

Customers should check the payment options shown during checkout before placing the order.
