# Document ID: POL-010
# Title: Warranty Policy

Warranty coverage depends on the product, manufacturer, seller, and warranty terms applicable to the order.

The warranty period begins according to the applicable warranty terms.

A warranty claim may require proof of purchase and product information.

The customer may be asked to provide serial numbers, photographs, videos, or other information required to assess the claim.

Warranty resolution may include repair, replacement, or another remedy specified by the applicable warranty terms.

Damage caused by misuse, unauthorized modification, or conditions excluded by the warranty may not be covered.

Customers should review the warranty information associated with the product before submitting a warranty claim.
