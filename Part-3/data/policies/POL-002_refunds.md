# Document ID: POL-002
# Title: Refund Policy

A refund is initiated after the returned product is received and the applicable verification process is completed.

The final refund amount depends on the amount eligible for refund under the order and return conditions.

Refunds are normally issued to the original payment method unless the applicable support process specifies another method.

The time required for a refund to appear may depend on the payment method and the financial institution.

Customers should retain the return or refund reference until the refund is reflected in the relevant payment account.

If a refund has been initiated but is not visible after the expected processing period, the customer should contact support with the order and refund reference.

A refund does not necessarily mean that the original delivery or convenience charges are refundable.
