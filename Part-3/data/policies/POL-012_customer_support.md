# Document ID: POL-012
# Title: Customer Support and Escalation Policy

Customers should first use the support option associated with the relevant order.

Providing the order ID and a clear description of the issue helps support identify the correct order and resolution path.

Support may request additional information to verify the issue.

Customers should provide requested evidence accurately and avoid submitting misleading information.

If an issue cannot be resolved through the initial support flow, the customer may request escalation through the available support channel.

The escalation process may require review of the order history and previously provided information.

Customers should retain relevant order, shipment, return, refund, and support reference numbers until the issue is resolved.
