# Document ID: POL-005
# Title: Delivery Policy

The estimated delivery date is shown on the order or shipment tracking information.

Delivery estimates may change because of operational conditions, transportation delays, weather, serviceability, or other fulfillment factors.

Customers can check shipment progress through the order tracking information.

If tracking shows that an order is delayed, the customer should first check the latest estimated delivery date.

If an order is marked delivered but the customer has not received it, the customer should contact support promptly and provide the order details.

Delivery to a particular address depends on the serviceability of that location.

A customer should ensure that the delivery address and contact information are correct before shipment progresses.
