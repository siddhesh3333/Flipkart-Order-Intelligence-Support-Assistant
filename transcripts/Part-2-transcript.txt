Project: Flipkart Order Intelligence & Support Assistant
Part: 2

Overview
- Part 2 focuses on product image classification for category recognition.
- The goal is to build a transfer-learning image classifier that can identify product categories and save the final model for Part 3 integration.

History
- The workflow follows a vision-model pipeline using a pretrained ResNet-18 backbone with a Fashion-MNIST-style classification head.
- The backbone is frozen and the classifier head is trained for the target taxonomy of product categories.
- The final saved model is then used by the Part-3 product-classification tool.

Work_done
- Built the transfer-learning model architecture in Part-2/model.py.
- Added preprocessing to normalize the image input pipeline in Part-2/preprocessing.py.
- Created dataset loading and training logic in Part-2/load_dataset.py and Part-2/train_head.py.
- Saved the trained classifier head and assembled the final full model in Part-2/save_final_model.py.
- Evaluated the trained model using Part-2/evaluate.py and confirmed the inference path is compatible with the part-3 tool contract.

Technical_details
- Base architecture: pretrained ResNet-18, ImageNet weights, adapted to 10 output classes.
- Labels correspond to Fashion-MNIST style categories: T-shirt/top, Trouser, Pullover, Dress, Coat, Sandal, Shirt, Sneaker, Bag, and Ankle boot.
- The model intentionally freezes the pretrained backbone and trains only the final classification head.
- The persisted final artifact is stored as models/product_classifier.pt.
- Preprocessing is designed to match the Part-3 classifier tool to avoid mismatches between the training and inference pipeline.
