Project: Flipkart Order Intelligence & Support Assistant
Part: 1

Overview
- Part 1 focuses on return-risk prediction for Flipkart-style order data.
- The goal is to build a reliable model that estimates the likelihood of a return and to save the trained artifact for downstream use by Part 3.

History
- The workflow begins with dataset profiling and preprocessing for the order dataset.
- Missing values and feature patterns were reviewed to understand the return-rate signal.
- A baseline logistic model and a Random Forest model were evaluated, with the Random Forest chosen as the final artifact for the Part-3 integration.

Work_done
- Analyzed the order dataset and confirmed the main problem shape: 6000 rows with 13 columns.
- Reviewed return-rate behavior and feature gaps, including missingness in rating_given and payment-method-related patterns.
- Trained and compared baseline and model-based classifiers.
- Selected the Random Forest pipeline and saved the final model and threshold metadata for reuse.
- Recorded acceptance summary details in Part-1/ACCEPTANCE.md.

Technical_details
- Dataset summary: 6000 rows, 13 columns, overall return rate around 22.75%.
- Missingness review: rating_given had material missing values; payment_method effects were important for missingness classification.
- Baseline testing: dummy baseline F1 (class = 1) was 0.0.
- Logistic model performance: ROC-AUC around 0.6251, F1 around 0.3921, with best threshold around 0.44 improving recall by about 17.95 pp.
- Random Forest was selected as the final model and saved to the project models directory.
- Feature importance analysis identified payment_method_COD and price_inr as major contributors, with customer_tenure_days and delivery_distance_km also important in subgroup checks.
- The saved threshold metadata is aligned with requirement-based selection and is used by the Part-3 tool layer.
